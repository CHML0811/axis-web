Health & fitness data (stays on your device)

If you grant permission, AXIS reads your sleep analysis and sleeping wrist temperature from Apple Health to calculate your sleep debt, and uses step count from Core Motion for the Wake Mission. This data is processed on your device and is never uploaded to our servers, never shared, and never used for advertising or tracking. You can revoke access anytime in the Health app or iOS Settings.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising or share it with any third party.
  • We do not show third-party ads in AXIS.

Third parties who process data for us

We use a small set of service providers strictly to operate the app: Apple (in-app purchases, HealthKit — on device), Firebase (authentication and sync), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Sentry (crash reporting). Each processes only the data described above, on our behalf.

AI features

When you use AI planning, the goal text you provide is sent to our backend and to a third-party large-language-model provider solely to generate your plan. We do not send your health data, identity, or contacts to the model.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account, or by emailing privacy@axis.app. Deletion is permanent.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email privacy@axis.app. We do not sell data, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

Children

AXIS is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
